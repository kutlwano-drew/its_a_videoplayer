# It’s a Video Player

A simple, fast, and distraction-free video player for desktop.

**It’s a Video Player** is designed for people who just want to open a video, press play, and watch. It keeps the interface clean and familiar while providing the controls you actually need for everyday video playback.

No unnecessary complexity. No feature overload. Just a solid video player.

---

## Features

- **Play your videos** — Open videos from your computer and start watching immediately.
- **Playlist support** — Add multiple videos and arrange them in the order you want.
- **Easy playlist reordering** — Drag videos into a different position whenever you want.
- **Save and load playlists** — Save your playlist and come back to it later.
- **Continue watching** — The player remembers where you stopped so you can continue later.
- **Repeat modes** — Repeat the current video, repeat the entire playlist, or turn repeat off.
- **Playback speed controls** — Speed up or slow down playback when you need to.
- **Simple volume controls** — Adjust volume quickly from the player.
- **Mute** — Instantly mute or restore audio.
- **Quick seeking** — Jump backward or forward through a video with simple controls.
- **A/B looping** — Select a section of a video and play that section repeatedly.
- **Fullscreen playback** — Watch your videos without distractions.
- **Mouse controls** — Useful playback actions are available directly through the mouse.
- **Keyboard controls** — Quickly control playback using familiar keyboard shortcuts.
- **Clean desktop interface** — A minimal interface that stays out of your way while you watch.
- **Dark interface** — Designed for comfortable video watching.
- **Built for desktop** — Designed specifically for Windows, Linux, and macOS.

## Why It’s a Video Player?

There are plenty of video players that can do almost everything.

That isn't always a good thing.

It’s a Video Player focuses on the things people actually need when watching videos, while keeping the interface simple enough that you don't need to learn how everything works.

Open a video.

Press play.

Watch.

The extra controls are there when you need them, without getting in the way when you don't.
